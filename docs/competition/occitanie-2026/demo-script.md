# Demonstration video script

Updated 29 October 2026. This is a recording plan, not evidence that a video has been recorded.

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Record the real macOS application at 1920 × 1080 or higher. Use actual outputs from the recorded build, not mock screens or fabricated results.

## Recording prerequisites

- Record the build's commit hash and keep its matching subject, correction and provenance files together.
- Keep notifications, personal files and API credentials off screen.
- Show the public V35 French workspace: five finite paper families, with optional repeat practice. It does not call an AI model.
- Prepare a standard or enlarged-print paper and the separate Occitanie activity. Keep their non-official notices visible.
- Do not present internal checks as teacher approval, observed learner timing or complete exam equivalence.
- Show app-owned pages only. Cite official reference links in the accompanying notes; do not reproduce official exam pages in a published video unless their reuse rights have been cleared separately.

## Shot list and narration

### 0:00-0:15: The app

Visual: title card, then the French workspace.

Narration: “Keldin creates non-official NSI practice papers on a Mac. The French catalogue has five paper families, each with a subject and proposed correction.”

### 0:15-0:35: Written structure

Visual: session 2027 information, the three exercises and the marks on a generated cover.

Narration: “The French path follows the announced 2027 written structure: three independent exercises, eighteen technical points and a separate two-point language component. Difficulty and duration have not been calibrated with learners.”

### 0:35-0:55: Local generation

Visual: choose standard or enlarged print, generate a paper and open its documents. Show the repeat-practice choice without clearing history.

Narration: “The app builds these papers locally from versioned questions, data and answers. It prefers unused families. You can choose repeat practice, but a repeat is not a new question family or evidence of AI-model quality.”

### 0:55-1:15: Reasoning and correction

Visual: one code or SQL task, its supplied data and the corresponding correction. Use an example covered by the retained checks.

Narration: “Tasks ask for traces, code construction and explanations based on the supplied data. Supported algorithm and SQL checks test the proposed answers. Internal review also compares syllabus coverage, task logic and printed pages with official sources.”

### 1:15-1:35: Printed pages

Visual: app-owned subject and correction pages, including readable code and an enlarged-print example. Put official comparison links in the notes, not official page images on screen.

Narration: “The PDFs use A4 pages and independent branding. They remain more compact than the official comparator. Matching a question count or a page layout would not establish equal difficulty.”

### 1:35-2:05: Occitanie activity

Visual: open the implemented regional activity and its correction. Show the duplicate school names, the case-sensitive Python filter, one SQL question and the source notice.

Narration: “A separate activity uses twelve school records from the Région Occitanie's licensed open dataset. Duplicate names and mixed capitalisation make concrete Python and SQL problems. This small extract is not representative of the whole region. Both PDFs credit the source and explain the limits.”

### 2:05-2:25: Planned work

Visual: the current English application draft and its twelve-month development plan. Do not display unfilled personal details.

Narration: “The proposed next work is testing on more Macs, accessibility checks and further regional data activities. Students receive ordinary PDFs, without needing a Mac or a local AI setup to read them. Funding and the final work plan remain the applicant's decisions.”

### 2:25-2:30: End card

Visual: Keldin icon, project title and links to the working application and evidence notes.

Narration: “Keldin: local NSI practice, with its current limits documented.”

## Short version

For a 35-second teaser, show the five-family workspace, one generated task and the twelve-record regional activity. State that repeat practice is optional and difficulty and duration are uncalibrated. Do not promise teacher reviews, a supervised pilot, prize funding or classroom outcomes.
