# Demonstration video script

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Record the real macOS application at 1920 × 1080 or higher. Do not use mock screens, fabricated model results or an unreviewed paper presented as approved.

## Recording prerequisites

- Use a stable committed build and record the commit hash.
- Keep notifications, personal files and API credentials off screen.
- Show the public V32 French route and its three-family limit. No AI model is needed.
- Prepare one French assessment whose subject, correction and manifest match the recorded implementation and catalogue identities.
- Mark the artifact as non-official and non-reviewed unless a real teacher review is attached to those exact hashes.

## Shot list and narration

### 0:00–0:15 — The problem

Visual: title card, then the French workspace landing screen.

Narration: “Keldin prepares non-official French NSI drafts for teachers to review. Its current catalogue contains three paper families. The proposed correction and checks stay attached to each draft.”

### 0:15–0:35 — French assessment rules

Visual: session 2027 information, three exercises, 3 h 30, the finite-family disclosure and non-official status.

Narration: “The French path is separate from the existing British paths. It uses the announced 2027 written structure: three independent exercises, eighteen technical points and a separate two-point language component.”

### 0:35–1:00 — Local generation

Visual: choose standard or enlarged print, create a draft, then open its documents and quality evidence. Do not reset originality history to force a repeated family.

Narration: “V32 builds its three exercises locally without calling an AI model. App-owned data drive the questions, proposed answers and exact credits. Repeated families are refused. These drafts do not establish model quality or classroom suitability.”

### 1:00–1:25 — Question quality

Visual: open a sustained case, its code or SQL task, the supplied data and the corresponding proposed correction.

Narration: “The cases progress through traces, state changes, construction and justification. Automated checks verify supported algorithms, SQL and exact totals. Difficulty labels and estimated times still need learner calibration.”

### 1:25–1:45 — PDF fidelity

Visual: show the generated subject beside the public 2026 Métropole reference, focusing on cover hierarchy, exercise headings and code typography. Keep independent branding visible.

Narration: “The A4 profile uses measured historical conventions and independent branding. These drafts remain more compact and table-led than the comparator. Page count and visual similarity do not prove equivalent demand.”

### 1:45–2:05 — Teacher decision

Visual: review panel, validation evidence and artifact hashes. Do not record a demonstration approval or impersonate a teacher.

Narration: “Automation is not examiner approval. A teacher sees unresolved limits and records a decision tied to the exact PDF hashes. Any change invalidates that review.”

### 2:05–2:25 — Occitanie pilot

Visual: simple diagram of teacher Mac to PDF to ENT, loRdi or paper; then the twelve-month pilot plan.

Narration: “For students, the result is an ordinary PDF on the ENT, loRdi or paper. The Prix Occitanie would fund two NSI reviewers, a supervised regional pilot, accessibility tests and an optional context module based on licensed regional open data.”

### 2:25–2:30 — Honest close

Visual: Keldin icon and project title.

Narration: “The technical prototype works. The prize would finance the evidence needed to decide whether it deserves classroom use.”

## Short version

For a 35-second teaser, show the limited catalogue, one draft, one PDF comparison and the review form. End with: “A local prototype for NSI practice drafts. Teacher review and a learner pilot are still required.”
