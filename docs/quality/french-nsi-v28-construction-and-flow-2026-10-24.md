# French NSI V28 construction and response flow

## Engineering changes

V28 addresses two observed limitations in V27: E2 relied heavily on tracing and single-condition repair, and graph correction tables could be separated from their questions. The official 2026 Métropole day-one paper develops several tasks from representation and tracing into complete functions, composition and justification (notably pages 7–10 and 12–13). This comparison informs the demand progression, not reuse of its questions or illustrations.

E2 retains the original app-owned incident data and SQL/state sequence. Question 2h now requires a complete `repartition_clos` function, with zero-count categories, one traversal, explicit input contracts and no mutation. Question 2l asks for the meaning, initialization and preservation of the count on a prefix of incidents, followed by an empty-input deduction. The reference solution is checked against independent counts for row permutations and category subsets. Quarter-point credit and minutes move from earlier tracing/reference tasks: E2 remains 70 minutes and its allocated technical total is unchanged. The three independent exercises still total 18 technical points, with a distinct 2-point indicative language assessment.

The V28 catalogue identity binds the new prompt, code, marking criteria and reasoning answer. Its selection reuses finite V24 scene identifiers but renders through a version-specific construction layer; package replay rejects changed answers or mixed catalogue identities. V13–V27 rendering and selection defaults remain unchanged. V28 preserves V27's corrected E3 security credit.

The opt-in V28 graph layout flattens nested keep-together blocks before grouping each material with its question. Normal-print correction groups include the answer and rubric. Large-print content can split when a complete group exceeds a page rather than overflowing. Runtime validation rejects a normal-print graph material separated from its question. No extra blank pages or target page count are introduced.

## Verification gates

Regression tests cover executable reference-code results, no input mutation, all three point profiles, exact marks/time, package tampering, historical replay, normal/large-print bounds and graph material/question association. A fresh source-pinned live diagnostic and rendered-page comparison are required before reporting the PDF result. Protected Backend/macOS checks remain mandatory before merge.

## Qualification limits

These are engineering improvements, not teacher-calibrated difficulty or timing. The new questions require independent teacher review and learner calibration. Rights clearance and accessibility qualification remain separate gates. No official PDFs are bundled, and no examiner, school, Apple or regional approval is claimed. The final cross-model matrix remains gated on a source freeze and satisfactory manual paper comparison.
