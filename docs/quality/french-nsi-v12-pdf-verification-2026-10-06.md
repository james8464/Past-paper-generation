## Publication gate and remaining gaps

The runtime now rejects a PDF if an extracted graph weight, tree cell, code/API, instruction, expected answer, or exact marking credit disagrees with the locked first-exercise package; subject answer leakage and rubric leakage also fail. Standard and large-print pairs are tested. This checks extraction and deterministic agreement, not pedagogical merit, semantic correctness outside the controlled exercise, genuine screen-reader navigation, print hardware, or official equivalence.

Do not label the French route validated until a pinned live complete paper survives all deterministic gates, every generated PDF is manually checked against its own data and relevant official references, all three exercises meet subject-quality criteria, independent French NSI teachers review the planned stratified set, and learner timing/difficulty and accessibility checks are recorded.
