# Assessment-objective calibration reference

Verified against the cited specifications and locally held official mark
schemes on 31 August 2026. This is implementation guidance, not evidence of
equivalent difficulty or completed subject-expert review.

## Meaning comes before totals

| Subject | Valid objectives | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| AQA Accounting 7127 | AO1, AO2, AO3 | AO3 includes analysis and evaluation. Familiar accounting techniques can earn AO1 even when they involve calculation. |
| AQA Computer Science 7517 / OCR Computer Science H446 | AO1, AO2, AO3 | Computational analysis belongs to AO2; AO3 includes designing, programming and evaluating solutions. AO3 does not universally require a prose evaluation. |
| Current Economics / AQA Business routes | AO1, AO2, AO3, AO4 | Preserve their separate analysis/evaluation objectives; do not apply the Accounting correction globally. |

Infer operations from the subject and actual task, not from a universal meaning
for an AO number. A wrong allocation must be corrected together with the task's
required work, credited answers, review prompts and exported/printed scheme.

## Component versus qualification percentages

| Component | Approximate within-paper AO1 / AO2 / AO3 percentages |
|---|---|
| AQA CS Paper 1 | 20 / 30 / 50 |
| AQA CS Paper 2 | 55 / 40 / 5 |
| OCR CS Paper 1 | 52.5 / 22.5 / 25 |
| OCR CS Paper 2 | 37.5 / 45 / 17.5 |

Each written CS component contributes 40% of its qualification. Divide the
published component shares by 0.40 before comparing them with a paper's raw
marks. These published approximate shares are not exact integer item tariffs
or empirical confidence intervals. Focused topic banks need valid objectives,
but must not inherit whole-paper percentage requirements.

Sources: [AQA 7517 assessment scheme](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/computer-science/a-level/computer-science-7517/specification/scheme-of-assessment);
[OCR H446 specification](https://www.ocr.org.uk/images/170844-specification-accredited-a-level-gce-computer-science-h446.pdf),
version 3.0, PDF page 23 / printed page 17. The local OCR scheme inventory spans
Autumn 2021 and June 2022–2024; those schemes do not uniformly supply per-item
AO allocations. Do not present inferred mappings as published board metadata.

## Current Accounting form pattern

The June 2025 AQA 7127 schemes match the current generators' mark sequences:

- Both Section A totals: AO1 = 30, including familiar short calculations.
- Both Section B totals: AO2 = 32, AO3 = 8.
- Each 25-mark decision: AO2 = 5, AO3 = 20.
- Each complete 120-mark form: AO1 = 30, AO2 = 42, AO3 = 48 (25/35/40%).

The published whole-qualification ranges are 24–26%, 33–35% and 40–42%.
Its component columns are shares of the qualification, not percentages of
each individual paper. Sources: [AQA 7127 assessment scheme](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/accounting/a-level/accounting-7127/specification/scheme-of-assessment);
local `AQA-71271-MS-JUN25.PDF`, pages 9–25, and
`AQA-71272-MS-JUN25.PDF`, pages 9–27.

## Edexcel short-response example

Local `9ec0-01-rms-20240815.pdf`, PDF pages 4 and 7, assigns
Knowledge2/Application1/Analysis1 to contextual four-mark explain-one-reason
items. Credit combines a relevant factor, its explanation and visible source
evidence. Neither a universal Knowledge2/Application2 split nor a universal
AO1/AO2/AO3 = 1/1/2 split correctly describes every four-mark question.

Keep selected-response subparts separate from neighbouring constructed
responses. Parent summaries must not duplicate marks. Extended choice questions
must be audited along candidate-answerable paths, not by adding all options.
