The app chooses the final edge of the post-first-closure minimum route as a second closed edge. This edge is distinct from the first closure and is not incident to A. On the existing graph family, a surviving A-to-F route exists; the app must prove that for each constructed seed rather than assume it. Equal-cost alternatives are allowed under the already printed alphabetical tie-break.
